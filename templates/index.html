The Workflow

Eliminating Manual Gate Passes

A completely digital four-tier verification chain from the village gate to the unloading platform.

1

Slot Registration

Farmers log in with their verified Kisan ID, enter tractor details, and book a staging window from home.

2

Moisture Clearance

Upon arrival, grain samples are digitally measured. Lots passing the 12% limit proceed instantly.

3

Automated Weighing

Gross vehicle weight is captured straight from the weighbridge sensor to the APMC ledger, halting tampering.

4

J-Form Settlement

The electronic J-Form is sent via SMS after tare weighing, initiating Direct Bank Transfer (DBT).
